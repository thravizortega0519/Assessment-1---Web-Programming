FRIENDS

FRIENDS is a popular American sitcom that follows the lives of six friends living in New York City. The show focuses on their relationships, careers, and everyday struggles while highlighting humor and friendship.

It aired from 1994 to 2004 and became one of the most loved TV shows worldwide.

Main Cast

  • Jennifer Aniston
  • Courteney Cox
  • Lisa Kudrow
  • Matt LeBlanc
  • Matthew Perry
  • David Schwimmer

Main Characters

  • Rachel Green – Fashion lover and waitress turned businesswoman
  • Monica Geller – Organized chef and perfectionist
  • Phoebe Buffay – Quirky musician with a unique personality
  • Joey Tribbiani – Funny actor who loves food
  • Chandler Bing – Sarcastic office worker
  • Ross Geller – Paleontologist and Monica’s brother

Reviews

FRIENDS received positive reviews for its humor, chemistry between cast members, and relatable stories. It remains one of the most iconic sitcoms of all time.
